Three Structures Under Downstream Finance

Choose the Right Structure for Every Flow.

Three ways to finance the invoices between an anchor and its vendors: Vendor Financing (VF), Purchase Invoice Discounting (PID) and Sales Invoice Discounting (SID). Here is what each one means and when to use it.

VFVendor Financing

FlexiLoans pays the vendor early against an invoice the anchor has acknowledged. The vendor borrows, and the anchor repays FlexiLoans on the due date.
